# LNT Excellence voice guide

How the site should sound: like one of the three founders explaining the business to a factory owner across a table. Plain, specific, and short.

## Rules

1. **Say who and what.** Name the thing: suppliers, stock, late deliveries, purchase orders. Avoid abstractions like "solutions", "operational challenges" or "moving parts".
2. **One idea per sentence, in a full sentence.** No two-fragment slogans ("Practical support. Clear next steps.").
3. **Describe the reader's week, not our virtues.** "A supplier ships late and your schedule slips" beats "we deliver meaningful results".
4. **Only claim what is true and checkable.** No invented clients, numbers, response times, prices or geography. If a fact is missing, ask the founders.
5. **Use "we" and "you".** Use founder names where a person did the thing.
6. **No all-caps taglines.** Small caps labels name the section and nothing more ("GET IN TOUCH").
7. **One call to action, worded one way:** "Start a conversation".

## Words to avoid

practical (as filler), stronger, excellence (outside the company name), solutions, challenges, journey, next chapter, empower, passionate, proven track record, sustainable growth, meaningful results, full potential, path forward, alongside you, clarity, confidence.

## Test before publishing

- Could a competitor paste this sentence on their site unchanged? If yes, add a detail or cut it.
- Would a founder say it out loud to a client? If not, rewrite it.
- Does any word from the avoid list appear more than once on the page?
